Animated ASCII scenes for any website

Maille draws sharks, koi, birds, mountains and the sea out of text characters, frame by frame, right in the browser. You tune a scene, bring in your own photo or a few words, and paste it into your site with one line. It is open source and free.

Why Maille

Motion on a website usually means a video file that softens when it scales, or a heavy library that does far more than you need. Maille takes another route: every scene is a small simulation drawn as characters on a canvas, so it stays sharp at any size, never plays back the same way twice, and costs a single script.

It can sit in a card, fill a hero, or swim behind your text, the way the sharks do on the edges of this page.

What you can do

Start from a scene

Sharks, mantas, koi, flocks of birds, the open sea, and an alpine range with its own sunrise and night. Each one moves on its own terms.

Bring your own

Drop in a photo and it gets a quiet motion of its own, or type a word and let it ripple. Your image never leaves your browser.

Tune every detail

Density, glyphs, colour, speed, glow, format, the number of creatures. Your settings are still there when you come back.

Take it anywhere

Copy the embed for any HTML page, Webflow or Framer, share an iframe link, or save a PNG or a video.

Embed

The Copy embed code button in Maille writes this for you, with your settings inside config. The scene fills its element, pauses when it is out of view, and shows a still frame to visitors who prefer reduced motion.

<script src="https://SITE/maille.js" defer></script>
<maille-scene preset="alpenglow" style="display:block;width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9"></maille-scene>

Iframe

For tools that only accept a URL, such as Notion or some page builders. The settings travel in the address.

<iframe src="https://SITE/e.html?preset=alpine-day"
        style="width:100%;aspect-ratio:16/9;border:0" loading="lazy"></iframe>
Attributes
AttributeValueEffect
preseta name aboveStart from a built-in scene.
configJSONSettings copied from Maille, merged on top of the preset.
speednumberPlayback speed, 1 is normal.
pausedpresent or absentHolds the current frame.
motionalwaysKeeps animating even when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
dprnumberCaps the pixel density, e.g. 1 for a full-page background.
JavaScript 
const scene = document.querySelector('maille-scene');
scene.set({ agDaylight: 1, agBirds: 9 });   // change any setting live
scene.pause(); scene.seek(12.5);             // exact frame at 12.5 s
scene.play();
scene.presence = 0.5;                        // share of cells drawn, for dissolves
scene.source = videoOrCanvas;                // image scene: draw any image, video or canvas

// Without the element
const engine = Maille.create({ scene: 'waves', width: 1920, height: 1080, cols: 160 });
engine.render(canvas.getContext('2d'), 3.2);
